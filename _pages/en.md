## Research Projects
{: #projects}

- **Evolutionary optimisation.** I developed a region-focused memetic algorithm with smart initialisation and tailored search operators for real-world waste collection routes with multiple depots, disposal facilities, and trips. I also study how co-evolution can improve ensemble fairness while maintaining predictive performance.
- **Vehicle trajectory prediction and uncertainty modelling.** I use high-definition map semantics and implicit scene context for interactive trajectory prediction. I have also studied how to account for uncertainty in deep neural network perception when planning vehicle motion.
- **Controllable driving scenario and motion generation.** AutoSceCraft generates road networks, traffic rules, roadside structures, and traffic flows from scratch for use in multiple driving simulators. CoMoGen combines vehicle placement, multimodal trajectory generation, and trajectory selection to generate diverse and controllable vehicle motion.
